### [1] [KernelOPT: Dispatch-Aware Agentic Search for GPU Kernel Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30059)
*Aheli Poddar,Sanskar Prasad,Arindam Samanta,Subha Chakraborty,Vishal Goyal,Rohit Singh Rathaur*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: KernelOPT是一个多智能体系统，将编译后的模型视为结构化产物，保留厂商库调用并仅优化生成的Triton子内核，通过四重验证级联端到端优化，在250个KernelBench问题上实现相对torch.compile的加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深度学习性能取决于GPU内核效率，而PyTorch Inductor等编译器自动生成的GPU内核常远逊于专家实现。现有LLM辅助内核优化器仅将编译模型当黑盒，孤立优化单个内核，忽略编译器的结构决策且不做端到端验证，因此无法系统性地提升整体模型性能。

Method: KernelOPT将编译模型视为结构化产物：保留cuBLAS、cuDNN等厂商库调用，仅针对生成的Triton子内核，使用五个由profiling引导的LLM智能体进行优化。采用四重验证级联——静态验证、多种子正确性、模型级float64回退验证和性能门控——在优化过程中筛选候选，并对重组后的模型做端到端验证。若无候选通过全部四重门，则保留编译器基线。系统支持PyTorch nn.Modules、独立Triton内核和Helion内核。

Result: 在250个KernelBench问题上，KernelOPT相对torch.compile取得几何平均加速：Level 1为1.40×（51/100），Level 2为1.15×（31/100），Level 3为1.07×（12/50）。

Conclusion: 通过尊重编译器结构决策并结合多智能体优化与严格端到端验证，KernelOPT能在多种任务与内核类型上稳定超越torch.compile，同时以基线保留机制保证可靠性。

Abstract: Deep learning inference and training performance depends critically on GPU kernel efficiency. Modern compilers such as PyTorch Inductor automatically generate GPU kernels from high-level model code, but frequently underperform expert-written implementations by wide margins. Recent LLM-assisted kernel optimizers can close this gap for standalone kernels, yet treat compiled models as black boxes, generally optimizing individual standalone kernels without respecting the compiler's structural decisions or verifying the model end-to-end. We present KernelOPT, a multi-agent system that treats compiled models as structured artifacts. It preserves vendor library calls (cuBLAS, cuDNN) and exclusively targets generated Triton sub-kernels using five profiling-guided LLM agents. A four-gate verification cascade of static validation, multi-seed correctness, model-level float64-fallback verification, and performance gating filters candidates during optimization and verifies the re-stitched model end-to-end. If no candidate passes all four gates, the system preserves the compiler baseline. The system accepts PyTorch nn.Modules, standalone Triton kernels, and Helion kernels. Evaluated on 250 KernelBench problems, KernelOPT achieves geometric mean speedups over \texttt{torch.compile} of 1.40$\times$ (Level 1: 51/100), 1.15$\times$ (Level 2: 31/100), and 1.07$\times$ (Level 3: 12/50) across all problems.

### [2] [Requirement-Bound Verified Commissioning: A Frozen Four-Billion-Parameter Local Model as a Candidate Generator under an External Acceptance Layer with Verification and Release Authority](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30219)
*Mehmet Iscan*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 提出面向机电调试中传感器坐标与极性绑定的验收协议，将候选生成与发布权限分离，用外部门控与密封语法判定，并用冻结的4B本地模型路由无解析支持的请求。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 机电调试中坐标与极性绑定易出现错误发布，需要把候选生成与发布决策分离，确保发布内容可由外部门控在密封语法下推导，以保证可靠性。

Method: 协议将候选生成与发布权限分离；无法由确定性解析器支持的请求路由至冻结的4B本地语言模型；仅当两个事实均能在密封语法下由外部门控推导时发布；对符合条件的任务向金标准用户请求唯一规范答案。在144个由隔离智能体上下文编写、无法访问门控/语法/实验计划的任务上，按预先固定的标准进行单次评估。

Result: 1) 分离测量候选生成与发布：22个被路由的不可回答任务中，21个出现了伪造的ready计划，且全部被拒绝；83次发布在无模型调用下被复现。2) 83次发布中未观察到错误发布，IID假设下单侧95% Clopper-Pearson上界为0.0354，低于5%阈值；但基准外seed 0的146次发布中随后记录到1次错误发布。3) 用户答案保护有限：96个可回答任务中仅13个将两事实绑定到原文；其余任务的431个配对中有169个错误答案被发布，含坐标排除失败。因资格由答案密钥确定，未测试可部署提问策略；未测量门控敏感性与真实用户行为。

Conclusion: 协议在基准上未出现错误发布，但基准外出现1次，且对错误用户答案的保护较弱，未验证可部署提问策略、门控敏感性与真实用户行为。

Abstract: An acceptance protocol is developed for sensor-coordinate and polarity binding in mechatronic commissioning. Candidate generation is separated from release authority. Requirements unsupported by a deterministic parser are routed to a frozen local language model with four billion parameters. Plans are released only when both facts can be derived by an external gate under a sealed grammar. One canonical answer is requested from a gold-standard user when eligible. The protocol was evaluated once under a criterion fixed before benchmark construction, on 144 tasks written by isolated agent contexts without access to the gate, grammar, or experimental plan. Three contributions are established. First, candidate generation and release decisions were measured separately. Fabricated ready plans were committed on 21 of 22 routed unanswerable tasks, and all were rejected. The same 83 releases were reproduced without model calls. Second, no false release was observed among 83 releases. A one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson upper bound of 0.0354 was obtained as a diagnostic under an independent-and-identically-distributed assumption, below the sealed 5% threshold. However, one false release was subsequently recorded among 146 releases outside the benchmark at seed 0. Third, protection against incorrect user answers was characterized. Both facts were bound from the original text on 13 of 96 answerable tasks. Incorrect answers were released in 169 of 431 pairings on the remaining tasks, including failures involving coordinate exclusion. A deployable questioning policy was not tested because eligibility was determined from the answer key. Gate sensitivity and real user behavior were not measured.
