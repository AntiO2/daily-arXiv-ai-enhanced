### [1] [Decentralized Dynamic Stable Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41912)
*Jing Chen,Mengtong Gao,Zhenhe Zhang*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 研究玩家可动态到达/离开、无时钟无通信的分散式算法下玩家最优稳定匹配(POSM)的计算，给出近似最优的自校准协议及未知偏好下的学习协议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现实中匹配系统（如推荐、分配平台）存在玩家动态到达与离开，且缺乏公共时钟、事件信息与直接通信，只有本地提议/接受结果，需要低成本地维持当前POSM。

Method: 以失配代价（实现匹配≠当前POSM的轮数）为度量；已知偏好时设计确定性多项式时间自校准协议，仅用本地时间与提议结果，以g控制恢复时间、N_g(T)控制迭代数；未知偏好时让玩家与臂从自身观测中学习偏好，得到分散式学习协议。

Result: 已知偏好：代价O(t_*+K^2 log T)，优于此前的O(T^{1-1/K}(log T)^{2/K})，并可推广为O(g(t_*)+K^2 N_g(T))；随机化协议下界Ω(t_*+N_g(T))，g(t)=t时算法在K^2因子内最优。未知偏好：期望代价O(t_*+K^4 Δ_min^{-2} log T)，静态下界表明对T与Δ_min依赖紧。

Conclusion: 该工作给出分散式动态POSM计算的近最优自校准协议，揭示恢复时间与渐进失配代价的权衡，并将结果扩展到双向未知偏好，理论上下界基本匹配。

Abstract: We study decentralized computation of the player-optimal stable matching (POSM) with unannounced player arrivals and departures. Without a common clock, event information, or direct communication, players propose to arms and observe only their own accept/reject outcomes. Mismatch cost counts rounds when the realized matching differs from the current POSM.
  For known preferences, we give a deterministic polynomial-time self-calibrating protocol with cost $O(t_*+K^2\log T)$, improving the previous bound $O(T^{1-1/K}(\log T)^{2/K})$. Here $K$ is the number of arms, $t_*$ the last event time, and $T$ the horizon. Players use only local time and proposal outcomes, knowing neither $t_*$ nor $T$. For any nondecreasing, polynomial-time-computable $g(t)=Ω(t)$, the protocol achieves $O(g(t_*)+K^2N_g(T))$. Here $g$ governs recovery time, while $N_g(T)$ counts iterations of $t\mapsto t+g(t)$ below $T$, exposing a tradeoff between recovery time and asymptotic mismatch cost.
  Any randomized protocol with recovery scale $g(t)\ge1$ and sublinear asymptotic mismatch cost has worst-case expected cost $Ω(t_*+N_g(T))$. Thus, with $g(t)=t$, our protocol is optimal up to a $K^2$ factor among sublinear-cost protocols with $O(t_*)$ recovery time.
  For unknown preferences on both sides, each player and arm learns its preferences from its own observations after matching. We give a decentralized learning protocol with expected cost $O(t_*+K^4Δ_{\mathrm{min}}^{-2}\log T)$, where $Δ_{\mathrm{min}}$ measures preference gaps. A complementary static-setting lower bound adapted from the literature shows that the dependence on $T$ and $Δ_{\mathrm{min}}$ is tight.
