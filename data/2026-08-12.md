### [1] [Hand-Written PTX Tensor-Core GEMM Kernels: A Multi-Precision Study on NVIDIA L4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10103)
*Matt J. Borowski,Blazej Osinski*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 本文在NVIDIA L4 GPU上对比了WMMA C++ API与手写PTX内核在不同精度（FP16、INT8、INT4）GEMM中的性能，发现FP16无加速，INT8有1.4-1.8x加速，INT4有2.9-4.3x加速，并指出性能主要受内存系统行为而非占用率影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大多数应用程序代码通过WMMA C++ API间接访问Tensor Cores，但高性能内核依赖底层PTX流水线。本文旨在回答一个实际问题：何时用PTX替换WMMA才能真正带来性能收益。

Method: 在NVIDIA L4 GPU（Ada, SM89）上进行受控单GPU实验，比较双缓冲WMMA基线与手写PTX GEMM内核家族，涵盖FP16、INT8、INT4三种精度及N=512到N=8192的方形问题规模，使用Nsight Compute进行全指标性能分析。

These results identify the precisions and operating regimes in which the additional complexity of hand-written PTX is justified.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

FaCTz provides a block-wise mode optimized for throughput and a speculative per-point mode optimized for compression ratio.
