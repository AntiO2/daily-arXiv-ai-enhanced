### [1] [Merge Hulls Without Copying Vertices: GPU Isotonic Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24038)
*Elena Raikova*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 提出用"分段列表"表示凸包合并的保序回归并行算法，避免逐层复制顶点，在GPU上大幅加速可微排序中的保序求解器。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 保序回归（isotonic regression）的标准算法PAV在序列内是串行的；torchsort对每条序列用单线程执行，长序列上很慢。已有思路是利用"拟合值为累积和的凸包斜率序列"、凸包可并行合并，但逐层合并会反复复制存活顶点，当大多数点都存活时总搬运量达Θ(n log n)，代价高。

Method: 将每个chunk的凸包只存储一次；合并后的凸包不复制顶点，而表示为"chunk凸包的分段列表"（每chunk至多一段），合并时仅更新该列表。论文证明了该表示的正确性，并界定了合并阶段的工作量与深度；实现为CUDA，在4块NVIDIA GPU上与"每层用整卡复制存活顶点"的对照实现比较。

Result: 在稠密凸包上，合并阶段快17–276倍，整体算子快3.9–19倍；在噪声数据（存活顶点少）上差异较小。在可微soft rank中用其替换torchsort的保序求解器后，T4上前向与反向快2.3–230倍，带Spearman损失的紧凑模型训练步快1.6–9倍。

Conclusion: 分段列表表示能有效消除逐层复制顶点的冗余开销，使凸包式保序回归的并行合并在大规模序列上显著加速，并为可微排序/概率校准提供更快的精确GPU求解器。

Abstract: Isotonic regression finds the monotone sequence closest to the data in weighted least squares. It is used to calibrate probabilities and inside differentiable ranking. Its standard algorithm, pool adjacent violators, is sequential within a sequence, and torchsort, the exact GPU solver of differentiable sorting libraries, runs it with one thread per sequence, which is slow on long ones. The fit is the slope sequence of the lower convex hull of cumulative sums, and hulls of sorted points can be merged in parallel. Merging partial hulls level by level, however, copies the surviving vertices at every level, which moves $Θ(n \log n)$ data when most points survive. We store each chunk's hull once and represent a merged hull as a list of segments of these chunk hulls, at most one per chunk; a merge then updates the list. We prove the representation correct, bound the work and depth of the merge phase, and measure a CUDA implementation on four NVIDIA GPUs against a control that copies the surviving vertices with the whole GPU at every level. On dense hulls the merge phase is 17 to 276 times faster with segment lists and the whole operator 3.9 to 19 times; on noisy data, where few vertices survive, the difference is small. In a differentiable soft rank, replacing torchsort's isotonic solver makes forward and backward 2.3 to 230 times faster on a T4, and the training step of a compact model with a Spearman loss 1.6 to 9 times.
